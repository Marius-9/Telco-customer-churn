# Telco Customer Churn Analysis

## Business Analytics Case Study

An interactive business analytics project focused on understanding customer churn in the telecommunications sector. The project uses customer data to identify key churn patterns, analyze high-risk customer segments, and propose targeted retention strategies.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Customer churn is a major challenge for telecommunications companies. Understanding why customers leave is essential for improving customer retention and reducing potential revenue loss.

The objective of this project is to analyze customer churn patterns and identify the main factors associated with customer attrition.

The analysis was conducted using **Microsoft Power BI** as part of the **Week 5 Business Analytics Case Study at AnalystLab Africa**.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

- Measure the overall customer churn rate.
- Identify the customer groups most affected by churn.
- Analyze churn according to contract type, services, payment methods and customer characteristics.
- Identify key factors associated with customer churn.
- Develop actionable recommendations to improve customer retention.

---

## 📊 Dataset

The analysis is based on the **Telco Customer Churn** dataset containing information on **7043 customers**.

The dataset includes variables related to:

- Customer demographics
- Tenure
- Contract type
- Monthly charges
- Payment method
- Internet service
- Technical support
- Online Security
- Online Backup
- Dependents
- Senior Citizen status
- Customer churn status

## 🔍 Key Findings

Several customer and service characteristics were associated with higher churn levels.

### Contract Type

Month-to-month customers represented the largest churn group, with **1655 churned customers**.

### Internet Service

Fiber optic customers accounted for **1297 churned customers**.

### Payment Method

Electronic check users represented **1071 churned customers**.

### Technical Support

Customers without Tech Support accounted for **1446 churned customers**.

These results indicate that contract type, service usage, payment method and support availability are important areas for further investigation and targeted retention actions.

> **Note:** The findings represent associations observed in the dataset and do not by themselves establish direct causation.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following actions are recommended:

1. **Encourage long-term contracts**  
   Offer loyalty incentives and attractive plans to encourage month-to-month customers to move to longer-term contracts.

2. **Strengthen early customer engagement**  
   Improve onboarding and follow-up during the first months of the customer relationship.

3. **Improve Internet service value**  
   Review pricing, service quality and customer experience, particularly for Fiber optic customers.

4. **Promote value-added services**  
   Increase awareness of Tech Support, Online Security and Online Backup.

5. **Develop targeted retention campaigns**  
   Use customer characteristics and service information to identify high-risk customers and provide personalized retention actions.
